# VU1 x native check

State, leads and fixes of the check. Docs index in `CLAUDE.md`; the method (per-chain collection, back faces, output format) is in `NATIVO.md`, "Runtime: VU1 vs native check". Disassembled microcode in `build/vu1/` (`tools/vu1dis.py`; game code, outside the repo).

<details>
<summary>How it works</summary>

- `WOTM_VU1CHECK=<s>` (runtime, `wotm_vu1check.inc`): every `<s>` s, in a fight frame, the VU1 executes the frame's chain. Each XGKICK triangle is matched (1 px) with the native one of the same object (`polyPkt`); each particle quad, with the one of its `fx[i]`. A geometry tie is broken by color.
- It compares geometry, texture (TEX0) and the shape of the particles. On the matched pairs, also the attributes (line `attributes:` and up to 8 objects `attributes pkt`):
  - `uv differs` (> 1 texel; `whole wrap` does not count), `color differs` (RGB > 4, the color the native side draws), `vertex alpha differs` (with ABE on both), `ABE differs`, `ALPHA differs`, `nonstandard TFX/TCC`;
  - `fog N (FGE differs, F differs)`: F of XYZF2 against `Scene::fogF`.
- Line `particles color` (vertex color and alpha of the matched particles, by type), line `lines` (`FX_LINE`, segment by segment), fans as triangles and, only in `--vu1cmp` with images, line `texels` (the texture of each TEX0 read from the GS VRAM at draw time against the one the native side decodes; diagnostics `WOTM_VU1CHECK_TEXELS=<id>` and `WOTM_VU1CHECK_TEXREC=1`). Method in `NATIVO.md`.
- **Limits of the method:** VU1 points are not compared (none in the corpus); for particles, ABE, ALPHA and UV are left out (only the vertex color and alpha); texels only in the replay with images. The PATH2/PATH3 HUD only enters through the inventory (count and box), without triangle comparison; the `hier` one is compared like the other objects. The fog is measured separately (mask from `WOTM_FOGSPLIT=2` on METRO: FA −107.9, FB 326,571, color 45/33/20; it matters from W ≈ 900 on).
- Counted separately: `repeated` (passes of the projected shadow), `shadow only` (tbp 3e60), `into a texture (shadow)` (FRAME outside the screen buffers 0x46/0x8C), `back faces via clipping`.
- **Inventory (`inv` lines):** what each side draws without a pair on the other, by owner, on any screen (the sampled frame goes from one `hier(0,0)` to the next, from any caller: `rtMain` in the fight, `userintMain` in the menus). On the reference side go the VU1 triangles and SPRITEs with no native object or `fx[]` (by MSCAL origin) and **all the PATH2 (DIRECT) and PATH3 (GIF channel) primitives**, grouped by path, type, texture, FRAME and ABE, with an on-screen box in pixels (`FULL SCREEN` = fade/clear; `into a texture` = FRAME off screen; `src` = the packet's origin in RAM, only in live PATH2). On the native side, the objects (including sky and icons) whose `polyPkt` no VU1 triangle touched, with `max alpha` (0 = the native side "draws" invisibly). Summary: `inv g=N reference only: G groups, P primitives (...) | native only: K objects, T tris on screen (...) | objects on both sides M`. **Names:** the texture comes out as `tex <id>` (index in `texInfo`, the number the native side uses; from the DBP of the upload packet), the origin in RAM as `psBorderDma+0x10` (table `wotm::symtab` generated in `wotm_syms.h` by `tools/native_syms.py`; no name = heap), and the HUD elements (`Scene::hud`, drawn by the native side and compared like the other objects) also come out in the line `inv HUD: ... by id (id:tris)`: in the fight, portrait, health and energy bars are id `0x1f47`. It is the list of what is missing or extra in the port, generated instead of remembered. Limit: the reference comes from the emulated VU1/GS; a bug of theirs shows up as a difference (the case of particle 0 of the debris, in "Explained, no bug on the native side").
- **Photos (`native/runtime/wotm_foto.inc`, `WOTM_FOTOS`; checkbox in the launcher):** so the owner can show a bug at the moment they see it. **F7** pauses (the EE thread stops at the capture point, right after `wotmCaptureFrame`: the finished frame stays on screen and the next one does not start; movie and sound keep going), **F6** advances one frame, **F8** takes the photo: the game runs until the check records a whole frame (from `hier(0,0)` to the next), stops on it and opens a text box; Enter saves the note. The photo is the frame **after** the one that was on screen at F8 (the screen switches to it before the box). Output in `<runtime>\fotos\<YYYY-MM-DD_HHMMSS>\`: `foto_NNN.vu1snap` (~43 MB, its own file, starts with record `A`), `foto_NNN_tela.png` (the native frame as it was in the window, with the hooks' fades, which the replay does not run) and `notas.txt` (number, `g_frame`, `currScreen`, loaded resources and the note). To investigate: `tools\vu1cmp.ps1 <folder>` redoes the check and the images of all the photos. The reference is still the runtime's VU1/GS: for "how it looks in retail", compare with PCSX2. Pause does not become `dt`: the frame with a stop counts 1 field (`wotm_overrides.cpp`) and the scheduler pushes the vsync deadlines by the paused time (`EeScheduler::shiftHostDeadlines`, in the patch); the watchdog ignores the pause. Without `hier(0,0)` (movie, loading) the photo gives up after 3 s. Delete resolved photos.
- **Triggers for menus:** `WOTM_VU1CHECK_WHEN=mem:<address>[=<value>]` (without a value: fires on every change of the word; `mem:0x6F8464` = one frame per menu screen, `currScreen`) and the **F8** key in the window (one frame right away, with no condition or cooldown).
- **Per-pixel diff with owner (`inv px` lines, only in the replay with images):** an owner map per pixel for each side (640x224, the last one drawn wins, no Z): reference with the PATH3 clear, all the XGKICK triangles and SPRITEs in the screen buffer (whole or not) and PATH2; native with the projected objects (including sky/icons, marked), the particles, the HUD on top (`HUD <id>`) and the text. Each pixel that differs becomes a pair `ref <owner> | nat <owner>` that is counted; `none` = background, clear or previous framebuffer. Example (character select): `ref pkt a1e080 tex 174 | nat none: 11029 px`.
- **Limits of the reference image:** it is the runtime's software GS, not PCSX2. The fight and menu reference shows text where the native side does not and vice versa: the native text comes from `fontPacket` read outside the sampled frame. `WOTM_VU1CMP_GORDER=1` reapplies all the GIF channel packets in the recorded order (the default only reapplies those without vertices).
- **False alarms (the check flags them, but they are not port bugs):**
  - **HUD:** both sides draw the same texture (`ref HUD 1f47 | nat HUD 1f47` in `luta_hud`); only a slight blur at the edges remains, from the way the texture is smoothed.
  - **`ScreenPolys` (flat HUD shapes):** the check lists `ref path3 sprites x2` as if only the reference drew them, because on that PS2 channel (PATH3) it only records a rectangle around them. The pixel map shows that the native side draws them (`nat HUD ScreenPolys`).
  - **Fades (darkening and brightening the screen):** they appear in the game. The `FULL SCREEN` ones count as reference-only because the native side receives the fade at the moment the game builds it, and the check's replay does not run that code. The native side does the screen clear by itself.
  - **Nearest ground and health marker:** the reference's software GS does not paint the nearest ground in some frames nor the health marker of `ScreenPolys` (T4HL at `tbp 1000`). PCSX2 shows both (`docs/screenshots/2026-10-15/2026-10-15_pcsx2_x_nativo_metro.png`).
  - The black band of the fight at `g_frame` 300 is the *letterbox*, not unpainted sky.
- GS SPRITEs of OBJECT (`objType` 2, `ObjSprite`): line `object sprites` (vu, native, matched at 1 px). They are removed from the `outside fx[]` count. In the dump, `objspr,vu|nat,x0,y0,x1,y1`. SPRITEs with FRAME outside the screen buffers (dust compositing) count as `into a texture`.
- `tools/lote.ps1` (batch runs) turns it on with `-Check 1`. `resumo.csv`: `casados_pior`, `textura_difere_pior`, `fx_casados_pior`, `vif1_fora_de_passo` (must be 0), `uv/cor/alfa/abe/alpha_difere_pior` (worst matched, worst texture differs, worst fx matched, VIF1 out of step, worst uv/color/alpha/ABE/ALPHA differs).
- `WOTM_VU1CHECK_DUMP=<prefix>` writes each check to `<prefix>_<g_frame>.csv`: triangles (position, TEX0, FBP, UV and RGBA per vertex, ABE, ALPHA, FGE, whole TEX0), `hdr` (per program: MSCAL address, `ffffffff` = MSCNT, and VU1 qwords 5–8 and 17–20; in program 0x15D8 also `ptl`, q169–q175 of the point lights), `lights` (`paraL`, `D_0025B238`, `specularColor`), particles (`fxcam`, `fxp`, `fxmat`) and origins of the SPRITEs outside `fx[]` (`rectorf`).
- Fog diagnostics: `WOTM_FOGSPLIT=1` (left half without), `=2` (mask; prints the parameters), `=3` (alternates per frame).
- **Without the game:** `WOTM_VU1CHECK_SNAP=<prefix>` writes the session's sampled frames to a `.vu1snap` (~36 MB the first, ~7 MB each following one; cap `WOTM_VU1CHECK_SNAP_MAX`); `WOTM_VU1CHECK_WHEN=fx:<type>|preset:<fxId>|frame:<a>-<b>` fires when the event is **born** (edge, not while it exists: the volcano smoke fires once; `fx:`/`preset:` only from `g_frame` 30 on and with the packet built in the frame, `info.attribs & 0x400`), `WOTM_VU1CHECK_BURST=<k>` records `k` consecutive frames and `WOTM_VU1CHECK_SHOTS=<n>` limits the shots. `tools\vu1cmp.ps1 <file or folder> [-Frame <g_frame>] [-NoPng]` redoes the check of each frame (all files in one runtime execution; ~1 s per frame with images), with the same output, without opening the game, and writes `<file>_<g_frame>_{ref,nat,diff,trio}.png` (ref = the runtime's software GS, an approximation; nat = native; diff = different pixels; details and limits in `NATIVO.md`, "Snapshot and check without the game"). To investigate a lead: trigger the event once with the trigger on, and run `vu1cmp.ps1` on the file after every change in `native/`.

<summary>Particle microprograms</summary>

Read in `build/vu1/particle.txt`; the sprine matches the packet in a dump (`build/dumps_live/ram_B1.bin`, fx 82). Common context (qwords 0–7, from 0x256B90): q0–q3 = `worldToScreenMat` transposed; q4 = ambient×0.3, `fogFA` in w; q5 = half the focal length (192; 90.24), `fogFB` in w; q6 = (−2048, −2048, −2049, 2048); q7 = (4095, 4095, 16777215, 255). CLIP: x and y in [0, 4096], W ≥ 1, and it disappears if F < 1.

VU generator: `RINIT` does R = 0x3F800000 | (bits(fs) & 0x7FFFFF); `RNEXT` does R = (((R<<1) ^ bit4(R) ^ bit22(R)) & 0x7FFFFF) | 0x3F800000 (float in [1, 2)).

**Sprine (type 8, entry 0x2770):**
- Memory: `pos[]` at qword 8; 32 colors in 264–295; UV in 296–299; tag at 300 (pa = `preAgeFactor` in x); parameters in 301–304; M2 = row 2 of `ParticleToEmitMat` (fx+0x2CD0) at 305; 306 = ((n−1)·N, age[n−1]·N, n−1). In the packet (RAM), q300–q306 are an UNPACK of 7 qwords with the data at `pos` − 0x74 (`sprinePacketBuild` rewrites 305 and 306), right before the UNPACK of `pos` (code at `pos` − 4); before them, UV in V4-16 (USN, 1/16 of a texel; data at `pos` − 0x98) and colors in V4-8 (USN; data at `pos` − 0x11C). q302.y–w and q306.x/z are integers.
- N = max(2, int(rate@0x2D8C + 0.5)), h = 1/N. q301 = (h·(numColors−1)/lifetime, h·expansionRate, —, f6), f6 = (max−min)/min if min > 0, otherwise 1. q302 = (minSize, N, (N+1)>>1, sprites per batch). q303 = h²·(0.75−0.5N; −1.75; 0.5N+1.25; −0.25); q304 = h²·(1.5; −3.5; 2.5; −0.5).
- Curve from `pos[n−1]` to `pos[0]`, one stretch per pair A = pos[i], B = pos[i−1] (Pm before, Pn after): two parabolas with C1 in the middle. Ghosts: Pm = B − 2·M2·((B−A)·M2) at the start (reflection of B in the plane through A normal to M2; the factor 2 comes from the MADDAw with vf16.w = 2), Pn = 2·pos[0] − pos[1] at the end.
- Forward differences (bit-exact): D1 = q303·(Pm,A,B,Pn), D2 = q304·(…), D2′ = q304 reversed (w,z,y,x)·(…). p = A; per step k: emits at p, p += D1, D1 += D2; after that, when N−1−k == (N+1)>>1 (and N > 2), D1 = D1 − c·D2 + c·D2′ and D2 = D2′ (c = 0.5 with even N, 0.125 odd; the drift with odd N is retail's). Each stretch restarts at pos[i]; pos[0] is not emitted: (n−1)·N sprites.
- Per global step s: a = age[n−1] + s/N; colorPos = min(31, (numColors−1)·a/lifetime·j), j = 1 + pa·(2 − r); color = lerp of the table, +0.5, trunc (on the 1st step of a stretch that does not start a batch, j enters twice). Side = (minSize + expansionRate·a)·(1 + (r−1)·f6), screen-aligned square. F = min(fogFA + fogFB/W, 255) on the 2nd vertex.
- R: RINIT(pos[n−1].w) and one RNEXT at the start; RINIT(pos[i].w) per stretch (it only restarts the generator: the current r, vf13.w, carries on); one RNEXT per step. The UV of step k uses r_{k+1} (sel = bits & 3: 0 mirrors U and V, 1 U, 2 V, 3 normal; du′ = du − 8, dv′ = dv − 8); j and side use r_k.
- Corners: center ± q5.xy·side/W = ± (192; 90.24)·side/W px, that is, half-side side/2 in the view plane; the 1st corner takes (u0, v0), the 2nd (u1, v1).
- GIF: PRIM 0x176 (SPRITE, TME, FGE, ABE, FST), REGS = RGBAQ, UV, XYZF2, UV, XYZF2; buffers 300 and 662.

**Electric arc (types 7 and 17, entry 0x19F8; `electricArcPacketInit/Build/Update` in `asm/common/partdraw.s`):**
- fx+0x2DC0 = seed, fx+0x2D9C = `normalScale` (amplitude), fx+0x2E50 = `segmentInitU` (texture phase in U).
- Memory: `pos[]` in chains at qword 8 (head: w = 1 + number of links; link: w = 5<<(level−1) or 0; 0 in the head ends it); 16 colors (float 0..255) in 138–153; tag at 154 (PRIM 0x7C: TRISTRIP, IIP, TME, FGE, ABE, STQ); 155 = (minSize, seed, segInitU, 138 + CLUToffset); 156 = (expansionRate, jag, segDeltaU, numColors−1). Requires n ≥ 3.
- Header at `pos − 0x28` (minSize, seed, segInitU, 138 + CLUToffset, expansionRate, jag, segDeltaU, numColors − 1) and colors at `pos − 0x13C`; the low w (16 bits, ILW) of the head is 1 + number of links, the link's is 5<<(L−1) (b is 2·w qwords from a, step 5 per point).
- RINIT(seed) per packet (R carries on between the chains). A link from a to b with level L > 0 becomes 2^L sub-stretches: amp = jag·|b − a| at the 1st level, half at each level; each gap gets the midpoint 0.5a + r·amp − 1.5·amp + 0.5b, with r = (rx, ry, rz): three RNEXT on entering the link, and after each midpoint rx = ry, ry = rz, rz = RNEXT.
- Ribbon (subroutine 0x1F70), in isotropic screen space with y down (y times fxh/fyh): ghosts P₋₁ = 2P₀ − P₁ and Pₙ = 2Pₙ₋₁ − Pₙ₋₂ (homogeneous); t_in and t_out unit; n = (t.y, −t.x); v = n_in + n_out + (t_out − t_in) if t_in × t_out < 0, otherwise + (t_in − t_out), without renormalizing. Vertices S ± v·(fxh·s_k/W, fyh·s_k/W) with fxh, fyh = half the focal length (q5): in the view plane, p ± (s_k/2)·v. s_k = minSize + exp·k/S; A (+) with V = 1, B (−) with V = 0; ST = (U_k/W, 1/W or 0, 1/W), U_k = segInitU + segDeltaU·(k − S/2); color = lerp in the table (clut + k·(nc − 1)/S, + 0.5, truncated); F = fogFA + fogFB/W. ADC outside the band, with W < 1 or F < 1; 0x2330/0x2390 handle a tip with W < 1 (the tangent goes up to the plane W = 0).

**Chain (type 12, entry 0x1D00, Ultra-V's rope):**
- Same ribbon 0x1F70, without subdivision. The traversal order comes from W(P₀) − W(Pₙ₋₁) (the MSUBz.w at the end of the header; the translation cancels out): negative, from Pₙ₋₁ to P₀ (LQD); otherwise, from P₀ to Pₙ₋₁. It starts at the farther end.
- At the tip of the rope several points coincide. The tangent between them is the zero vector: the RSQRT of 0 gives Q = maximum and 0 · max = 0. The product t_in × t_out comes out +0, "not negative", and v = n + (t_in − t_out). On the native side (y up) that is the cross < 0 branch when the order is the same and the other one when it is reversed (`rev` in `Particles`).
- The reference depends on the emulated VU1 not using FMA: the `ps2_vu1_*.cpp` compile with `/fp:precise`, like the native VU0 (seam in the patch's CMake). With `/fp:fast /arch:AVX2` MADDq becomes FMA, and `vf3·q − round(vf3·q)` leaves 1 ulp of noise in the zero tangent, which once normalized draws a crooked ghost link.

<details>
<summary>Explained</summary>

- **Shadow only on the receiver with the right cs (Kineticlops):** each `psBlkLst` entry (stride 0x30) writes the receiver cs at `+0x14` (`psBlockerSetShadowCs`), and `psObjectCull` only lets an object receive the shadow if that cs is the same as that of the traversal that emits it (0 for the stage). Most blockers point to the stage. Kineticlops points to the cs of a quad flush with the ground (tex 324, blend 0x48, flags 0x8010); projecting onto the stage floor as well draws the eye's shadow twice. Each `drawList` item keeps the traversal's cs (`DrawItem::cs`) and `drawShadows` only projects onto items with the blocker's cs (`ShadowBlocker::shadowCs`). The quad is only in front of the floor because of its `zBufferFudge` (1.0122), so receivers with their own cs use the fudge; on the stage floor layers (fudge 1.0012/1.0024) the fudge darkens the shadow beyond the reference, which is why it stays only on those with their own cs. Some blockers with their own cs (monsters in split screen and in `shell_logo`) are left without a receiver in the frame; per the asm, retail does the same. `WOTM_SHADOWANYCS=1` projects onto everything; `WOTM_SHADOWLOG=1` lists blockers and receivers.

- **Alpha above 1 in the common blend:** the GS lets As go above 1 (At·Av/128 up to 255; ~300 objects per frame with As ≈ 1.03, texture 0xFF and vertex 0x42, and a few with As ≈ 2), and in the common blend it does Cs·As − Cd·(As − 1), clamped to 0..255. The objects where As can go above 1 (the highest vertex alpha times the highest of the texture, `MeshData::maxA` and `texAmax_`) come out in three passes of the same geometry, each pixel deciding in the shader (modes 3 to 5): the common blend with alpha 0 where As > 1; `Cs − Cd·(1 − 1/As)` only where As > 1 (`gsBlendOver1`, subtraction); and the destination times As (`gsBlendOver2`, output As − 1 times `DST_COLOR`, plus the destination). The 8-bit framebuffer only rounds result/As (error < 1). The sky dome is left out: the reference does not show the gain on it (the destination under the dome is the previous frame, the same sky, and the math converges to Cs), and over the black clear it would come out ×1.027. To measure the effect, scale the native side by each frame's brightness factor (GS math in the shader, `WOTM_GSTRUNC`): without discounting that, brightening an object always increases the error. `luta_3ia` (girder tower and building) and `congar_olhos` end up slightly further from the reference with the three passes; the rest of the corpus, closer. `WOTM_NOOVER=1` clamps As to 1.

- **Large skinned meshes (`goliath_flare`, Assboss):** the check links each VU1 program to the native object whose packet contains the program's origin. The skinned packet is a chain of sub-packets whose size is only known by decoding (Goliath's is ~0x29000), so `decodeObject` stores the end of the chain (`MeshData::bytes`) and the check uses that size; with a smaller fixed size, the programs at the end become triangles without an owner, with the same triangles left over on the native side.

- **Lens flare outside the `particles` line:** the sprites of the lens flare packet (`lensFlarePacket`, an fx[] of type 11 that the particle collection does not pick up) are compared in the `lens flare` line; `flareSys` removes them from the `particles` line so they are not counted as "fx N preset 0 type 0 ...: vu 2 native 0".

- **METRO pedestrians** (fx 91–99): it is the fog, not the instant. The VU1 discards the particle with F < 1. On METRO, `vu1FogParams` = (−107.9; 255; 1; 326,600) (`build/dumps_live/ram_energy_robot.bin`), so F = −107.9 + 326,600/W < 1 with W > ~3,000 (the `particleDraw` list goes from farthest to nearest, which is why the discarded ones are always the first ones, fx 91 and 92). On stage 1 the cutoff is around 16 thousand. The native side applies the fog to the particles. Total losses (0/1000) with "into a texture (shadow)" jumping from ~570 to ~5,900 indicate a wrong FRAME (`sceGsSetDefLoadImage`, #311): the check discards the particles as render-to-texture.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Explained, no bug on the native side</summary>

- **Menus, `tex 174` "reference only":** it is the drive-in's sky dome (OBJECT `a1df80`, sphere with radius ~14,900, flags 0x0210), which the native side draws and matches triangle by triangle. The game clears the screen with black (the two full-screen SPRITEs, RGBAQ 0/0/0/0x80) and the sky lets the background show through, so the native side's clear has to be black (with another color, the sky comes out tinted). The `nat none` of the owner map does not mean the native side does not paint: the map is rasterized by the check, separately from the image.
- **Select screen text:** the fight and menu reference shows text where the native side does not and vice versa ("player 1" on the select screen): the native text comes from `fontPacket` read outside the sampled frame. It is a limitation of the sample. On screen, on the character select (1 player, down, 3 X), retail on PCSX2 and the live native side show the same texts and marks ("player 1", "cpu 1", the color boxes 1 to 4 and the cursor "1" on Togera's icon) (`docs/screenshots/2026-10-16/2026-10-16_selecao_texto_pcsx2_vs_nativo.png`). The pad script needs `-Step 2.5` for the 3rd X to arrive after the screen accepts input.
- **Debris particle 0 (the runtime's VU1):** in the prologue of microprogram 0x1180, the pair `MADDq.w vf0, …` (upper with destination vf0, only for the MAC flag of the "behind the camera" test) + `DIV Q, vf0w, vf6w` (lower) needs the DIV to read vf0.w = 1.0. If the interpreter runs upper before lower without putting the vf0 destination into shadowing (`upperVfShadowReg`), the DIV reads the vf0.w that upper has just written: the Q of particle 0 comes out ~140 times larger (0.0676 instead of 1/2058), the two corners go past +x and +y, and `FCAND 0x3ef` discards it (clip 0x145); the following ones use the Q of the loop's DIV and pass. The runtime handles the case (the VU1 emits n sprites per system; `destrocos.vu1snap`: `frag` 15 of 15 in the 6 frames).
- **Menu alpha objects "native only" (`tex 75/76` flags 0x8a00 and `tex 142/81/162` flags 0x8210, screens 1, 2, 12, 15, 6 and 8):** the end of `hier(0,0)` leaves the last packet, with the alpha OT (`hierAlphaBins` → `hierAlphaPkt`), to `hierFlushObjQ`, called by `hierDmaHandler` when the VIF1 DMA completes. On the PS2 that happens before `hier(0,1)`: in the menus `userintMain` calls `rtWaitForVu1`, which waits for `objsInPacket | objsInAlphaPacket` to reach zero; in the fight the wait is the one in `particleDraw`. Without the VU1, `main.cpp` cuts `rtWaitForVu1` (no-op), and `hier(0,1)` would reset `hierObjPkt` and the OT with the 8 objects still pending. In the fight the `particleDraw` hook dispatches the OT. In the runtime seam (`main.cpp`, `hook_waitVu1`, in `wotm-runtime.patch`), in the sampled frame (`g_wotmVu1Tap`), `rtWaitForVu1` calls the original `hierFlushObjQ` when there is still a pending packet, playing the role of the interrupt, at the same moment as on the PS2 (before the letterbox and the fade that `userintMain` sends through PATH3); outside the check it does nothing. Menu snapshots captured without that hook do not have the OT.

- **Aiming reticle as a SPRITE "without an owner" (`tex 319` in `luta_hud`, `tex 322` in `destrocos`):** the reticle (`Monster::m_reticleCS`, `_cs` in the active list) points to a SWITCH whose byte +8 the game writes with `m_reticleState` (child 0: green ring `tex 322`; child 1: yellow ring `tex 319`), each child an additive GROUP (`ALPHA_1` 0x48, `TEST` 0x5040C) with an OBJECT sprite (`objType` 2, 64x64 T4 textures in the heap next to the monster). `hier(v,0)` (before `TheGame::Update`) descends into it with `drawMe` 1 and puts it in the alpha OT; the `Update` of the same frame turns `drawMe` off (the reticle disappears), and the alpha OT only goes out in a chain of `hier(v,1)`, whose native collection (RAM at that instant) no longer has the object. The check completes the collection of each later chain with the objects from the 1st collection that the chain references (`chainTap`); in `luta_hud`, "object sprites: vu 177 native 177 matched 177". The native side draws it like the GS: in the `viewer` with `drawMe` on, the ring adds on average 45 and at most 64 per channel (color 0x80 0x80 0, vertex alpha 0x20, texture alpha up to 0xFF: As up to 0.5). Live, the native side captures at the end of the frame (`timerWaitForMinUpdateRate`), so it shows the reticle by the state after `Update`, one frame before the PS2 shows or removes it. Diagnostics: `WOTM_TEXINFO=<id>` also prints the object's sprites; `WOTM_VU1CMP_RAM` writes the RAM of the replay frame.
- **Electric arc, ~2.5% of the VU1 without a pair and ~6% extra on the native side:** METRO, Robo-47 against 3 Robo-47, 40 s dump. By the arc's length on screen:
  - up to 10 px: native 1,028 quads, all with a pair at 1 px; VU1 868 quads and 104 loose triangles;
  - 10 to 30 px: 8 without a pair out of 634;
  - above 30 px: 428 of 428.
  It is the check: `degenerate()` discards from the VU1 the triangles with area < 0.02 px² (a distant arc quad is smaller than 1 px), and not from the native side. The microprogram's clipping is applied on the native side (`arcQuads`, per-triangle mask of the strip with the band [0, 4096] and W ≥ 1, diagonal B_k–A_k+1), without changing the measurement.
- **7 (pattern B):** the alpha items attached to the monsters sometimes come out 1–2.5 px off: the packet carries the matrices from the instant of the traversal, and no collection falls there. The check also tries the collection at `hier(0, 1)`. **`wake_parede` (96.2%, 134 missing and 147 extra) is this:** all the unpaired ones are from two skinned monsters in the alpha OT (`cb23e0` tex 140 and `cbb190` tex 139, flags 0x8203/0x8a01), ~75 per monster, in a single matrix batch of each (programs 199–209 and 147–157). The whole batch comes out shifted uniformly, (−1.0; −0.3) px from the VU1 to the native side; the other batches match with an offset of ~0. A bone that moved between the traversal and the end of the frame.
- **9, `shadow only`:** the projected shadow pass redraws back faces that the base discards, and thin triangles create ambiguity.
- **10 (tex 156/193):** the numbers are the object's texture set (`node+0x50`), of several static buildings on stage 1. The swap is always between the common 128x128 texture (cbp 1bd2) and a 64x64 one of the same object, 1 or 2 triangles per check (372 of 4,959). Case dissected in `f1_1697.csv` (pkt d7e980): the VU1 triangle is a sliver (0.01 px²) on the edge between the two sections, and its three vertices match a single vertex of another native sliver. `match()` does not require a one-to-one correspondence. The true native twin falls below the `degenerate()` threshold (|area2| 0.0029 < 0.02), while the VU1 one passes (0.0232), because the GS rounds to 1/16 px. Shuffling the VU1 order, the error appears in 10 of 20 orders. In the 52 `f1_*` dumps, 1 pair with a different texture in 520 thousand. In the check one could quantize the native side to 1/16 px in `project()` and require distinct vertices in `match()` (not applied).
- **14 (tex 242/243):** a distant building made of tiny triangles; at 1 px, almost every VU1 triangle has 4+ native ones in the same place. 94% to 99.6% have an identical native one in place; the rest is ambiguity.
